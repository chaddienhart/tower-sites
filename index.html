About Us

Tower Sites Inc. was established in 1997 and has grown to manage a network of two dozen owned or managed towers and rooftops, supported by a full-time staff of experienced professionals. Our team offers an integrated suite of services, boasting extensive experience in site acquisition, planning & zoning, tower & site construction, and tower space leasing, providing unmatched practical experience from initial scouting to tower stacking. We specialize in providing prime tower sites for co-location in challenging zoning areas throughout Wisconsin and the Upper Midwest, offering build-to-suit tower sites if needed. Our deep local knowledge and experience with area municipalities provide a competitive advantage to help speed your company's build-out goals.
